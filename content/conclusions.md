## Impact and conclusions
{:#conclusions}

While we have seen rapid advancements around intelligent agents in recent years,
human intelligence is not increasing at this rate, and may even be decreasing,
so we appear to be reaching a crossing point where AI can surpass HI.
While the Semantic Web technology stack gave us a foundation for intelligent agents,
this paper argues that it also provides the foundation for *data integration across brain knowledge and external knowledge*,
achievable through Semantic Brain-Computer Interfaces.
Work towards resulting Symbiotic Knowledge Graphs is not only scientifically interesting,
but may also be important for humanity from an existential perspective.

While part of our vision could already be achieved by current BCI technology,
other parts still require future BCI breakthroughs, for which this vision sets out specific goals.
Long-term, such deep integration of human-external knowledge can significantly impact society, for example:

- **Societal collaboration** becomes more efficient, through semantically aligned thoughts, memories, and intents.
- **Education** is transformed, as knowledge can be accessed rather than memorized.
- **Researchers** can solve complex problems through better reasoning capabilities.
