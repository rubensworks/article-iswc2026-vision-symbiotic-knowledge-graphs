## Impact and conclusions
{:#conclusions}

While we have seen rapid advancements around intelligent agents in recent years,
the intelligence of humans is not increasing at this rate,
and may even be decreasing.
Currently, we appear to be reaching a crossing point where AI can surpass HI.
While the Semantic Web technology stack gave us a foundation for intelligent agents,
this paper argues that it also provides the foundation for *data integration across human brain knowledge and external knowledge*,
which can be achieved through Semantic Brain-Computer Interfaces.
While work towards resulting Symbiotic Knowledge Graphs is not only interesting from a scientific perspective,
it may also be considered important for humanity from an existential perspective.

While part of our vision could already be achieved by current BCI technology,
other parts still require future BCI breakthroughs,
for which this vision sets out specific goals.
Long-term, such a deep integration of human-external knowledge can significantly impact our society at different levels, for example:

- **Societal collaboration** among humans can happen more efficiently, through semantically aligned thoughts, memories, and intents.
- **Education** is transformed, as new knowledge can directly be accessed rather than memorized.
- **Researchers** can grasp and solve more complex problems thanks to increased reasoning capabilities.
